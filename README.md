# 🚀 Space Shooter

A simple 2D arcade-style space shooter built with the **Godot Engine** and **GDScript**. Control your ship, dodge incoming hazards, and blast through waves of enemies!

---

## 🎮 Features

- **Player Controls:** Smooth movement and shooting mechanics.
- **Enemy System:** Spawning enemy waves and hazards (asteroids / enemy ships).
- **Collision & Combat:** Bullets, damage detection, and explosion effects.
- **UI & Scoring:** Dynamic Heads-Up Display (HUD) showing current score and health.
- **Audio & Visuals:** Retro sound effects and background scrolling.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Engine:** [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/) (v4.x)
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Assets:** Open-source 2D space sprites & SFX *(e.g., Kenney.nl)*
